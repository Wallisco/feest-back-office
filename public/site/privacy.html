This policy explains what information the FEEST Driver app and our driver services collect about you, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and your rights under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

This policy covers drivers who use the FEEST Driver app. Customers who order on FEEST and the stores that sell on it are covered by separate notices.

The app keeps your sign-in in your phone's secure storage. It does not access your contacts, microphone, other apps, or photos you didn't take in the app.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your personal information.
  • We don't show you adverts or share your information with advertisers.
  • We don't track your location when you're offline.

Who we share it with

  • Customers: your first name, vehicle and live location during their delivery only. The tracking link is released when you collect the order and stops when the delivery ends.
  • Stores: your first name and when you'll arrive, for orders you collect from them.
  • Our ordering partner, which runs WhatsApp ordering for stores: the delivery status and tracking details customers need.
  • Payout and wallet providers: what's needed to pay your earnings into your wallet.
  • ScootHero, if you rent a bike from them: your earnings and the rental deductions agreed with you.
  • Service providers who host and run our systems for us, under contracts that require them to protect your information.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

Where it's stored

Our systems run on servers in South Africa. If a service provider stores information outside South Africa, we only use providers that give the protection POPIA requires.

How we protect it

Information is encrypted between the app and our servers. Sign-in tokens are stored hashed. Staff only see what their role needs, and access to personal information is logged.

Your rights

Under POPIA you can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, and object to how we use it. You can turn off location at any time in your phone's settings, but you can't receive or complete deliveries without it. Email {{privacyEmail}} and we'll respond within 30 days.

If you're not happy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa): inforegulator.org.za/complaints.

Age

You must be 18 or older to drive with FEEST.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and tell you in the app before the change applies.
